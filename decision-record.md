## What a stakeholder will ask

To start I thought of some typical stakeholder questions

a. How many active customers did we have last month, by treatment and country?
b. Of the customers acquired in January 2023, what share were still active 12 months later?
c. What's our monthly churn rate, and how many churned customers came back?
d. How long does a typical customer stay? How long are they away before coming back?
e. Which customers were active between two specific dates?
