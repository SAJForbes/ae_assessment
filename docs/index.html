Customer retention

New and returning customers above the line; contracts ended below

Monthly churn rate

Share of the month's active customers whose contract ended that month

Retention by acquisition treatment

Share of each cohort still active, by months since first subscribing

Definitions

Active
Covered by at least one subscription on any day of the month. The number of subscriptions doesn't matter.
Contract end (churn)
Cover stops and doesn't restart within 28 days. Recorded in the month cover stopped.
New
The month a customer's first subscription starts.
Returning
The month a customer starts again after an earlier contract ended.
Cohort
The month a customer first subscribed. Treatment is the category of their first order.
Data window
January 2019 to 31 July 2024. August 2024 is excluded as a partial, fragmented extract. Churn for the latest month isn't final, because a contract end is only confirmed 28 days after cover stops.
